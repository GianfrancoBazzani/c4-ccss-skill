# CCSS v9 Testing Procedures

## Testing procedure definitions

**Review.** Reviewing documentation such as policy, standards, and procedures is the most common evidence- gathering technique. It is generally the first task of the CCSSA to help ascertain the people, processes, and technology components of an information system and ISMS that are in scope for the CCSS audit. NOTE: concern should be raised by both the CCSSA and CCSSA-PR if an audited entity points to external documentation as their policy or standard. For example, an audited entity points to an open- source configuration guide on the project website for a mechanism or library as their standard. This should not be acceptable as the audited entity should ensure that internal documentation exists and not rely on external documentation outside their control. a. A Policy is a document that defines the goals or "mission" of the entity, such as "we provide a secure environment for our customers to transact." b. A Standard is a document that will outline the best practices, based on internationally recognized standards, as to how the entity will meet this goal, such as "the entity implements strong access control mechanisms that conform to ISO27001 or NIST 800-53" and another example "the entity uses an HSM certified to FIPS 140-3." c. A Procedure is a document that defines exactly how a goal is to be achieved. A procedure document will list the steps or tasks involved to achieve a result. For example, a procedure document can be used to onboard a new customer or configure a new HSM.

**Interview.** Interviewing personnel responsible for and managing the in-scope systems is a vital evidence-gathering technique for a CCSSA. Interviewing personnel can highlight key information that is never located within written documentation, such as how work is really done- sometimes known as "Shadow IT." The key focus of interviewing personnel is understanding if the person interviewed is following the entity's policy/standards/procedures or deviating from these. Deviations will concern the CCSSA and the entity, as there will not be any oversight or control of what is actually being done and could introduce vulnerabilities into the in-scope environment.

**Inspection.** Inspection is an evidence-gathering technique where the CCSSA inspects system configurations and other technology components to ensure that configuration requirements stated in the standards and procedure documents have been implemented. For example, the entity states in their access control standard that all access into the in-scope environment must use MFA. The CCSSA should inspect the access control systems to ensure MFA has been implemented, configured correctly, and applied to all user accounts with access to the in-scope environment. Inspection is a "trust but verify" approach to auditing where the documentation and interviewees state a specific action has been taken, but the auditor needs to verify by inspecting the system.

**Observation.** Observing a process being undertaken is another important evidence-gathering technique along the same lines as inspection: trust but verify. The CCSSA, if possible, should observe the processes being undertaken to ensure they meet the documented processes. For example, the CCSSA can observe personnel reviewing audit log files from in-scope systems or the process of adding a new user account to a system. When observing a process is impractical, such as asking the audited entity to undertake a key creation ceremony in production, the auditor should instead ask the personnel to discuss the steps that would be taken.

## Testing procedures by requirement

### Aspect 1.01

#### 1.01.1.1

**Requirement.** Key material is generated by the actor who will be using it.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards and procedures for the generation of key material to ensure key material is generated by the actor who will use the key material.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the key material generation process, sometimes referred to as "key ceremony report" to ensure the key material is generated by the actor who will use it. NOTE: BAU records for the generation of key material are not expected for key material generated for one transaction, such as a wallet address created once for a deposit of funds into a customer's account.
- **Inspect:** Inspection for this requirement is optional; may be used at the CCSSA's discretion
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the key material generation process to ensure that the actor generating the key material will be the actor using the key material. Note requirement 1.01.1.2 as an exception.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of key material to ensure that the policies and procedures for the generation of key material are followed.

#### 1.01.1.2

**Requirement.** Where an automated signing agent will use key material, and the place of generation of key material is different from the place of use. The following criteria are addressed: 1. The key material is generated within a secure Key Management System that meets applicable CCSS requirements. 2. The key material is transferred securely to the automated signing agent from the place of generation that meets applicable CCSS requirements. 3. The key material is securely removed from the place of generation that meets applicable CCSS requirements.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of key material to ensure this requirements criteria is addressed.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the key material generation process, sometimes referred to as a "key ceremony report" to ensure this requirements criteria is addressed. NOTE: BAU records for the generation of key material are not expected for key material generated for one transaction, such as a wallet address created once for a deposit of funds into a customer's account.
- **Inspect:** Inspection for this requirement is optional; may be used at the CCSSA's discretion
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the key material generation process to ensure that the actor generating the key material will be the actor using the key material. Note requirement 1.01.1.2 is an exception.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of key material to ensure that the policies and procedures for the generation of key material are followed.

#### 1.01.1.3

**Requirement.** A digital signature for the key material generation mechanism is generated, published, and validated prior to each execution.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of key material to ensure a digital signature is generated, published and validated prior to each execution.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the key material generation process, sometimes referred to as "key ceremony report". The report should contain confirmation that the key material generation mechanism software was validated before use.
- **Inspect:** Inspection for this requirement is optional; may be used at the CCSSA's discretion
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the validation process to ensure key material creation policies, standards, and processes are followed.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the validation process to ensure key material creation policies, standards and processes are followed.

#### 1.01.2.1

**Requirement.** The methodology for generating key material is validated prior to use. Software does not include features that restrict which values can be used. Software does not include features that store or transmit data to another actor, unless that feature enhances security.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of key material to ensure that software used for key management processes is validated piror to use and does not include features that will restrict the security of the functions and mechanisms for generating key material or transmitting or storing key material to another actor.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the key material generation process, sometimes referred to as "key ceremony reports." The report should confirm that the key material generation mechanism methodology was validated before use.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the configuration of the key material generation mechanism to ensure the configuration parameters and values do not impact the security of the key material generation process.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the validation process to ensure key material creation policies, standards, and processes are followed.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the validation process to ensure key material creation policies, standards, and processes are followed.

#### 1.01.2.2

**Requirement.** In cases where key material is generated without the use of software, the generation methodology is validated to ensure determinism is not present.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of key material to ensure that non-digital key material generation methodology does not introduce determinism in the key material generation process.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the key material generation process, sometimes referred to as a "key ceremony report' to ensure no determinism was present during the key material generation process. For this requirement, the considerations should also be addressed and documented in the key ceremony report. NOTE: BAU records for the generation of key material are not expected for key material generated for one transaction, such as a wallet address created once for a deposit of funds into a customer's account.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the non-digital mechanisms used to generate key material to ensure key material creation policies, standards, and processes are followed.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the usage of the non-digital mechanism used to generate key material to ensure key material creation policies, standards, and processes are followed.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of key material to ensure key material creation policies, standards and processes are followed.

#### 1.01.3.1

**Requirement.** The generation mechanism for key material conforms to NIST SP 800-90A.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of key material to ensure that the mechanisms that generate key material conform to NIST SP 800-90A.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the key material generation process, sometimes referred to as a "key ceremony report." The CCSSA will review the reports to ensure the process conforms to NIST SP 800-90A.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the configuration of the key material generation mechanism to ensure that the configuration parameters and values conform to NIST SP 800-90A. The CCSSA will inspect the configuration of the key material generation mechanism to ensure that the configuration parameters and values conform to NIST SP 800-90A.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the key material generation process to ensure the process conforms to NIST SP 800-90A.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of key material to ensure key material creation policies, standards, and processes are followed.

#### 1.01.3.2

**Requirement.** Requirement: The key material generation process has been documented and addresses the following: 1. The key material generation process must be conducted in a secure environment. 2. The physical environment that will be used for the key material generation process is checked before use for any unauthorized recording/surveillance equipment, windows allowing the key material generation process to be viewed by external personnel, poor physical separation of the area from the main working areas, effective physical access controls to restrict unauthorized access of personnel, backup power supply, environmental controls to protect against electromagnetic interference, sound leakage, or other vulnerabilities that could compromise the process. 3. All equipment and software used for the key material generation process must be checked before use for updates such as new software versions, any signs of tampering, and be in good working order. 4. All moveable equipment such as hardware devices, laptops, and key material is secured from unauthorized access when not in use. 5. A detailed runbook defines all steps performed during the key material generation process. After the completion of each step, the participating actors sign off in the runbook stating that the step was performed and checked. 6. All roles participating in the key material generation process must be defined and utilized. Segregation of duties must be considered when allocating personnel to roles. 7. Each actor involved in the key material generation process must independently generate their own discrete key material. In a multi-signer scheme, no single actor is permitted to generate key material that will be used by another actor unless the actor is an automated signing agent. (Refer to requirements 1.01.1.1 and 1.01.1.2.)

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the creation of key material to ensure the criteria for this requirement is addressed.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review pre-generation checklists or equivalent that address criteria two, three, and four of this requirement and also requirement 1.01.1.3. A key material generation "runbook" or equivalent must address: Details of the participants, such as the names, roles, and contact information of the individuals involved, such as key custodians, witnesses, and auditors. Purpose of the key material ceremony such as the generation, activation, backup, or destruction of key material. A step-by-step account of the actions taken during the ceremony, including the use of non-digital mechanisms to generate key material. Details of the hardware and software utilized during the ceremony, including serial numbers and configurations. Information about the key material generated, such as key type, size, seed, and purpose (without revealing sensitive key material information). Description of the security controls in place to ensure the integrity and confidentiality of the process. The signatures of participants and witnesses affirming their roles and agreement that the ceremony was conducted according to the defined protocols. Date and time of the ceremony.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the devices and systems used for key material generation to ensure they are patched and that all critical security patches are applied no later than one month after the patch(s) are released.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA may want to observe the physical controls and environment where key material is generated either via a remote video call or while onsite. The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the key material generation process to ensure all criteria for this requirement are met.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the key material generation process.

#### 1.01.4.1

**Requirement.** Key material is generated on a Key Management System with sufficient entropy to ensure key material is not generated with any bias towards a reduced range of values, or other deterministic properties.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of key material that sufficient entropy is required when generating key material.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the key material generation process, sometimes referred to as a "key ceremony report," to ensure key material creation policies, standards and processes are followed.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the key material generation mechanism to ensure the entropy sources comply with this requirement. For guidance, the CCSSA may consult NIST 800-90B or equivalent.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the key material generation process to ensure the entropy sources meet the intent of this requirement.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of key material to ensure key material creation policies, standards, and processes are followed.

### Aspect 1.02

#### 1.02.1.1

**Requirement.** When considering the application of a single-signer mechanism to a wallet, the following criteria are addressed: 1. The criticality of the wallet to the CCSS Trusted Environment. 2. The impact of loss of customer funds controlled by the wallet. 3. The risk of a wallet compromise is included in the threat model defined in requirement 2.03.2.1. 4. The effectiveness of the security controls implemented to protect the wallet.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of a wallet to ensure that any consideration of using a single-signer mechanism must address the criteria within this requirement.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the generation of a wallet using a single-signer mechanism and ensure that all criteria for this requirement have been addressed and recorded in writing.
- **Inspect:** Inspection for this requirement is optional; may be used at the CCSSA's discretion.
- **Observe:** Observation for this requirement is optional; may be used at the CCSSA's discretion.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of a wallet to ensure this requirement's criteria are addressed when the entity considers using a single-signer mechanism.
- **Additional Guidance:** Review penetration test reports -- addresses point 4 Review Key Inventory- addresses criteria 1 Review Risk Assessment Report- addresses criteria 2 and 3

#### 1.02.2.1

**Requirement.** A wallet that has implemented a multi-signer mechanism has at least one redundant key for recovery purposes.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of a wallet to ensure redundant key material is required for wallets that implement a multi-signer mechanism.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the generation of a wallet to ensure there is redundant key material or equivalent that has been configured for the wallet.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the wallet generation mechanism configurations to ensure that there are no restrictions on the ability to assign redundant key material or equivalent to a wallet.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the wallet generation process to ensure redundant key material or equivalent is configured for a wallet.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of a wallet to ensure redundant key material or equivalent is generated for a wallet.
- **Additional Guidance:** Backup processes and procedures documentation may provide additional information

#### 1.02.3.1

**Requirement.** Key materials for a wallet that implements a multi-signer mechanism are stored in different locations.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of a wallet to ensure risks of storing key material in the same location are addressed.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the generation of a wallet to ensure that key material for a wallet that implements a multi-signer mechanism is stored in different locations.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the logical locations where key material has been recorded as stored to ensure key material for a wallet that has implemented a multi-signer mechanism is stored in different locations. The CCSSA may decide to inspect the physical locations where key material has been recorded as stored to ensure key material for a wallet that has implemented a multi-signer mechanism is stored in different locations.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the wallet generation process to ensure that if the wallet implements a multi-signer mechanism, it includes distributing key material to different locations.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of a wallet to ensure risks of storing key material in the same location are addressed.

#### 1.02.4.1

**Requirement.** Key material for a wallet that implements a multi-signer mechanism is stored by distinct operators within the entity or separate entities.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures for the generation of a wallet to ensure this requirement has been addressed.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review records of the generation of a wallet to ensure that key material for a wallet that implements a multi-signer mechanism are stored by distinct operators if applicable. NOTE: The use of an automated signing agent needs to be considered when addressing this requirement hence the use of "if applicable."
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the logical locations where key material has been recorded and stored to ensure that distinct operators control key material for a wallet that has implemented a multi-signer mechanism. The CCSSA may decide to inspect the physical locations where key material has been recorded as stored to ensure that distinct operators control key material for a wallet that has implemented a multi-signer mechanism.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the wallet generation process to ensure the process includes distributing key material to distinct operators if the wallet implements a multi-signer mechanism.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of a wallet to ensure this requirement has been addressed.
- **Additional Guidance:** NOTE: The use of an automated signing agent needs to be considered when addressing this requirement.

#### 1.02.5.1

**Requirement.** The entity has a documented policy in place which details the company's internal policies and procedures and covers the relevant areas of wallet generation.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards and procedures for the generation of a wallet.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review wallet generation records to ensure that the generation of a wallet follows the defined policies, standards, and procedures.
- **Inspect:** Inspection for this requirement is optional; may be used at the CCSSA's discretion
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the wallet generation process to ensure the process follows the documented policies and procedures for wallet generation.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel involved in the generation of a wallet to ensure the process follows the documented policies and procedures for wallet generation.

### Aspect 1.03

#### 1.03.1.1

**Requirement.** Key material is stored with the use of strong encryption when not in use.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards and procedures for requiring and ensuring operational key material is encrypted when not in use.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review key material generation records to ensure operational key material is encrypted when not in use.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the storage mechanism configurations that store the operational key material to ensure the configurations meet the defined policy, standards, and procedures.
- **Observe:** Observation for this requirement is optional; may be used at the CCSSA's discretion.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel responsible for ensuring that operational key material is encrypted when not in use.

#### 1.03.2.1

**Requirement.** A backup(s) of the operational key material exists.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards and procedures for requiring and ensuring operational key material is backed up.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review key material backup records to ensure operational key material is backed up.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the backup mechanism configurations that store the backup of the operational key material to ensure the configurations meet the defined policies, standards, and procedures. The CCSSA will inspect the storage containers that store the backup of the operational key material to ensure backups of the operational key material exist.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the backup process for operational key material to ensure operational key material is backed up correctly.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel who are responsible for backing up operational key material to ensure that backups are performed.

#### 1.03.2.2

**Requirement.** A backup(s) exists for all key material used in the wallet. The backup can take any form (e.g., paper, digital, metal).

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards and procedures for requiring and ensuring all key material is backed up.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review key material backup records to ensure all key material is backed up.
- **Inspect:** The CCSSA will inspect the backup mechanism configurations that store the backup of all key material to ensure the configurations meet the defined policies, standards, and procedures. The CCSSA will inspect the storage containers that store the backup of all key material to ensure backups of the operational key material exist.
- **Observe:** The CCSSA will request a demonstration of the backup process for operational key material to ensure all key material is backed up.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel who are responsible for the backup of key material.

#### 1.03.3.1

**Requirement.** The backup(s) is protected against environmental risks.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards and procedures for requiring and ensuring all backups containing key material are protected from environmental risks.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review relevant certification records for premises that store key material backups, such as certifications covering the inspection and certification of building fire protection controls, earthquake protection, and electrical surge protection.
- **Inspect:** For backups containing key material stored in physical containers such as bank safes, safe deposit boxes, and other physical storage facilities, the CCSSA will inspect the controls that protect the physical backups to ensure the backups are protected from environmental risks.
- **Observe:** Observation for this requirement is optional; may be used at the CCSSA's discretion.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel who are responsible for the backup of key material to ensure that environmental risks are identified and addressed.

#### 1.03.3.2

**Requirement.** The backup(s) of key material is stored in a geographically separate location(s) from the storage and usage of operational key material.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards and procedures for requiring and ensuring all backups containing operational key material are stored in geographically separate locations from the operational key material.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review the key material inventory, or other relevant BAU records that show backups containing operational key material are not stored in the same geolocation as the storage of operational key material.
- **Inspect:** For backups containing key material that are electronically stored, such as on a backup server, the CCSSA will inspect the storage areas to ensure that the backups are not stored in the same geolocation as the operational key material. For backups containing key material that are electronically stored, such as on a backup server, inspect the mechanism responsible for backing up the operational key material and storing the backup in the storage container. The CCSSA may request to inspect physical containers such as bank safes, safe deposit boxes, and other physical storage facilities to ensure all recorded physical backups are present or in transit to or from the physical storage facility.
- **Observe:** Observation for this requirement is optional; may be used at the CCSSA's discretion.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel who are responsible for the backup of key material to ensure backups containing operational key material are not stored in the same geolocation as operational key material.
- **Additional Guidance:** Under Inspection: The CCSSA may request to inspect physical containers, including bank safes and safe deposit boxes, to verify that all recorded physical backups are stored at the disaster recovery site (DR site).

#### 1.03.4.1

**Requirement.** The backup(s) is protected by access controls that prevent unauthorized parties from accessing it.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management and access control policies, standards, and procedures to ensure access control, including the use of the least privilege and need-to-know principles, is required for backups containing operational key material.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** If the backup media is physical, such as removable electronic media, paper, wood or metal, the CCSSA will review the following: 1. The list of personnel with access to key material and the codes/passwords/physical keys used to access the backup media (safe, locked drawer, etc...). 2. The access log mechanism that records all access to the backup. Access log records must contain: (a) the full name of the person who accessed the backup, (b) the date and time of access, (c) the reason for accessing the backup and (d) written management approval to access the backup. 3. Check if the backups containing key material can be sent to another premise, such as third-party premises. If they are sent, review the process for sending them. A written log recording the movement and authorization to transport the backup must be kept. The transportation mechanism of the backup media should allow for complete tracking while in transit. 4. If CCTV is present to monitor the location of the backup media, then review the CCTV video footage to ensure it is working correctly and retaining video records. If the backup is stored electronically, the CCSSA will review the following: 1. The list of personnel with access to the backups. 2. The access log mechanism that records all access to the backup. The access log records must contain: (a) the full name of the person who accessed the backup, (b) the date and time of access, (c) the reason for accessing the backup and (d) written management approval to access the backup.
- **Inspect:** If the backup media is physical, such as removable electronic media, paper, wood or metal, the CCSSA may inspect the following: 1. The access controls that protect the physical backups such as locked rooms/cabinets/containers, door access controls, locks and CCTV. If the backup is stored virtually, the CCSSA will inspect the following: 1. The access control mechanism configurations to ensure that only authorized personnel can access the backup files. 2. The user accounts with access to the backup files to ensure only personnel with roles required to access backup files have access.
- **Observe:** If the backup media is physical, such as removable electronic media, paper, wood or metal, the CCSSA may observe the following: 1. The physical locations storing backup media to ensure physical access controls have been implemented. 2. The location of any codes/passwords/physical keys of the backup media (safe, locked drawer, etc...) to ensure physical access controls have been implemented. 3. The transportation process of the backup media if moved between locations. If the backup is stored electronically, the CCSSA will observe the following: 1. Personnel accessing the backup files to ensure access controls have been implemented.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel who are responsible for access control to the backups containing operational key material to ensure that effective access controls are implemented.

#### 1.03.5.1

**Requirement.** The backup(s) implements some form of tamper-evident mechanism that allows an operator to determine if it has been accessed.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management and access control policies, standards, and procedures to ensure mechanisms that detect and record attempted access of a backup are required for backups containing operation key material.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** If the backup media is physical, such as removable electronic media, paper, wood or metal, the CCSSA will review the following: 1. The CCSSA will review the key material inventory or other relevant BAU records that show backups containing operational key material are stored within a tamper-evident mechanism, such as a tamper-evident container. If the backup is stored electronically, the CCSSA will review the following: 1. Any alerts generated by the mechanism configured to monitor and alert on access to the electronic backups.
- **Inspect:** If the backup media is physical, such as removable electronic media, paper, wood or metal, the CCSSA may inspect the following: 1. The tamper-evident mechanism used to store the backup containing operational key material to ensure the tamper evident mechanism meets industry specification requirements. If the backup is stored electronically, the CCSSA will inspect the following: 1. The logical security control configurations that detect and alert on access attempts to the backups containing operational key material to ensure the security control is configured correctly.
- **Observe:** If the backup media is physical, such as removable electronic media, paper, wood or metal, the CCSSA may observe the following: 1. The physical backup to ensure the backup media is stored within a tamper-evident mechanism. If the backup is stored electronically, the CCSSA will observe the following: 1. Personnel attempting to access the backup to observe the mechanism monitoring the backups generating alerts for the attempted access.
- **Interview:** The CCSSA will interview personnel who are responsible for ensuring that backups containing operational key material are protected by a tamper-evident mechanism.
- **Additional Guidance:** For example, tamper-evident bags that indicate ....tampering...

#### 1.03.6.1

**Requirement.** Backups of key material are stored with the use of strong encryption that is equal or greater to the security prescribed for the operational key material.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review key management policies, standards, and procedures to ensure the electronic backup(s) containing operational key material is encrypted with strong encryption.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review key material backup records to ensure all key material is encrypted with strong encryption.

### Aspect 1.04

#### 1.04.1.1

**Requirement.** The entity maintains checklists that cover all tasks that are completed when personnel vacate or transition into key holder roles within the entity. These checklists have been reviewed by knowledgeable personnel to ensure "least privilege principles" are applied to the system, as well as necessary access where required.

- **Review - Policy, Standards, Procedures (PSP):** The CCSSA will review access management policies, standards, and procedures to ensure grant and revoke processes are documented.
- **Review - Business As Usual (BAU):** The CCSSA will review grant and revoke request documentation to ensure that the documented processes are followed.
